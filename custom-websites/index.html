FAQ

The questions everyone asks first

Why is this so much cheaper than an agency?

No account managers, no bloated CMS, no server to maintain. Static sites on a CDN cost pennies to run and take a fraction of the time to build well.

What if I need changes later?

The $10/mo plan covers hosting and security. The $30/mo plan adds an hour of edits every month — text, photos, hours, anything short of a full redesign.

Do I own the site?

Yes. The domain is yours, the code is yours. The monthly plan covers hosting and upkeep, not a license to your own site.

Can you match my existing branding?

Send your logo, colors, and any brand guide you have — the build is fitted to your business, not a template with your name swapped in.
