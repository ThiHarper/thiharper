About

Hi, I'm Thi.

I letter escort cards, stationery, and keepsakes for weddings — with black dip-pen ink, a steady hand, and a soft spot for getting every name right.

How I Work

Progress over perfection

Weddings move fast, and I'd rather keep things moving than get stuck on process. That means quick, itemized answers when you need them, questions asked early if something's unclear, and a finished product that looks effortless — even when it wasn't.

I catch the small things before they become guest-day problems: a duplicate name across two tables, an ambiguous last name, a title that doesn't quite fit. If something needs your input, I'll ask — clearly, and only when it matters.
