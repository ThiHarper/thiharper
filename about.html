About

Hi, I'm Thi.

I'm a calligraphy and lettering artist based in Brighton, Michigan, specializing in custom event stationery, dip-pen calligraphy, and hand-lettered keepsakes.

I work full-time in software development, so I've spent years training my eye to notice the smallest details. Calligraphy started during COVID as a quiet creative outlet, a way to step away from screens and make something real with my hands during a tough time.

What began as a personal way to unwind quickly turned into a passion for letterforms, clean layouts, and the genuine joy of seeing a client's reaction to their finished piece. I love hearing how happy a recipient was to receive a custom gift I got to help create. Today, I bring that same patience and detail to dip-pen calligraphy, wedding escort cards, and personalized lettering on surfaces like cardstock, acrylic, canvas, and seasonal items like pumpkins.

Beyond the Ink
