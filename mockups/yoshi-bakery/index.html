Yoshi Bakery

Small-batch dog treats, baked fresh with human-grade ingredients — because your best friend deserves better than a bag off the shelf.

Why Yoshi Bakery

It started with one very good boy who turned his nose up at anything store-bought. A few years of recipe testing later, Yoshi Bakery bakes small batches of dog treats every week using the same ingredients you'd find in your own kitchen — peanut butter, pumpkin, oats, no fillers, no mystery meat. Every batch is baked to order and picked up (or shipped) fresh.

What We Bake

A rotating menu of treats, always made in small batches so nothing sits on a shelf.

Weekly Treat Box

A rotating assortment of fresh-baked treats, delivered or ready for pickup every Friday.

Birthday & Gotcha Day Cakes

Custom-decorated pup cakes for birthdays, adoption anniversaries, or any excuse to celebrate.

Wholesale for Groomers & Shops

Bulk batches for local groomers, boutiques, and pet stores who want fresh, local treats on their shelves.

Order or Ask a Question

Tell us about your dog (allergies welcome) and we'll help you pick the right box.
