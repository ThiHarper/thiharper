Privacy Policy

Effective September 9, 2026

Overview

Thi Harper Calligraphy ("I," "me," or "my") is a Michigan-based calligraphy and lettering business operating this website at thiharper.com. This policy explains what information I collect through the site, how it's used, and who I share it with.

Information I Collect

When you submit the Contact form, the email signup form, or place an order through the shopping cart, I collect what you choose to provide, which may include:

  • Your name, email address, and phone number
  • Event date, service or product of interest, and guest or item count
  • The message, personalization text, or design details you enter
  • A shipping address, if your order is being shipped

If online payment is enabled, payment is processed directly by Stripe. I do not see or store your full card number — Stripe handles that securely under its own privacy practices.

How I Use Your Information

I use the information you provide to respond to inquiries, prepare quotes, confirm and fulfill orders, and communicate with you about your order (including pickup or shipping details). If you sign up for email updates, I use your email to occasionally let you know about new services or seasonal openings — nothing more.

Third-Party Services

This site relies on a few outside services to operate:

  • Formspree — receives and stores Contact form and email signup submissions. See Formspree's privacy policy.
  • Stripe — processes online payments when checkout is available. See Stripe's privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics — provides aggregate, cookie-free visitor analytics (page views, general location); it does not track you individually or across other sites. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts this website.

Cookies

This site does not set its own cookies. If your order is processed through Stripe Checkout, that page (hosted by Stripe, off this site) may set its own cookies under Stripe's policy.

Data Retention

Form submissions are retained in Formspree per its own retention practices. I keep order and payment records as needed for bookkeeping and tax purposes.

Your Choices

You can ask me to delete information I hold about you, or unsubscribe from email updates, at any time by reaching out through the Contact page.

Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to This Policy

I may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top will reflect the most recent change.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out through the Contact page.
